# Disclaimer

[Русский](DISCLAIMER.md)

**MedControl is not a medical device** and has not been certified as medical software.
It is a personal tool for keeping a treatment history: it stores and shows the data you put into it.

**The AI assistant is not a doctor.** The texts a model generates (reviews of reports, comments on lab results, visit
questions, answers to your questions) can be incomplete, inaccurate or wrong. They are there to help you prepare for a
conversation with your treating doctor, not to replace it. Every decision about treatment, doses, pauses and tests is
the doctor's.

**Alert rules are an example, not a clinical standard.** The thresholds in the alert rules and in the treatment
protocol presets were written by one patient for their own case and have not been reviewed by a doctor. No alert does
not mean everything is fine; an alert is not a diagnosis. If you feel unwell, contact a doctor, not the program.

**Parsers and unit conversion can be wrong.** Check the values that were read against the original PDF, especially
after uploading a report from a new laboratory.

**The data stays with you.** MedControl runs on your own server or computer; the authors have no access to your data.
Data leaves it only if you connect an AI provider, Telegram, a cloud backup or an external AI agent (MCP) yourself.
You are responsible for backups, passwords, outside access and for what you send to those services.

**Lab report samples.** If you send a sample of a report from a new laboratory, only the anonymized text, the name of
the laboratory and the program version are sent, and only when you act: you read the text, tick the box and press
"Send". The PDF itself is not sent. The automatic anonymization may have missed something, so check the text yourself
before sending.
