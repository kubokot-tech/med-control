# Why I made MedControl

MedControl began during my own long treatment.

At some point I realised that keeping my whole medical history in my head was becoming almost impossible. Results from different labs, scans, prescriptions, drug changes, doctors' consultations. There was more and more information, and it was harder and harder to put it together into a single picture.

I started building a program for myself.

And this matters: **MedControl's features came from problems I run into during my own treatment.** I did not try to imagine what a patient might need. I solved specific problems that I had.

I have been using MedControl for eight months now. It has become part of my daily life and really does help me follow my treatment.

Over time I thought that a tool like this could be useful to others: people going through long treatment, and the relatives who help them.

That is why MedControl is free, its source code is open, and medical data stays under the user's control.

I know my experience is not universal. That is exactly why I am especially interested in how MedControl works for other people, what they are missing and what should change.

If you use the program, tell me whether it helps you. For me that is the most valuable feedback.

And if you would like to support the project, there is a way to do that too. It is entirely optional. **MedControl has been free and will stay free.**
